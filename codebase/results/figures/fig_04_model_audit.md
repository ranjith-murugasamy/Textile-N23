**What it shows.** Five versions of R-squared for each response: the value printed in the draft, the R-squared obtained by applying the printed Table 4 equation to the Table 3 data, and refits with linear plus two-factor terms, the full quadratic model, and the leave-one-out predicted R-squared of the quadratic model.

**Key takeaway.** For tenacity the printed equation reproduces only R2 = 0.45 of the variance against the 0.98 reported; the linear + 2FI refit gives 0.857 and the quadratic refit 0.975, which is close to the reported value, so the reported R2 values most likely came from a quadratic model whose squared terms were left out of the printed equations.

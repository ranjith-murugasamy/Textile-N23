**What it shows.** Left: the 15 Box-Behnken runs in coded factor space, twelve edge-midpoint runs and three centre replicates. Right: observed tenacity against hairiness for every run, coloured by the printed blend label.

**How to read it.** Read the left panel as the geometry of the design: no run sits at a corner of the cube. In the right panel, each dot is one yarn; points lower right are better (stronger, less hairy).

**Key takeaway.** Tenacity and hairiness are almost perfectly anti-correlated across the 15 runs (Pearson r = -0.983); the 70:30 label gives the strongest and least hairy yarns, the 30:70 label the weakest and hairiest.

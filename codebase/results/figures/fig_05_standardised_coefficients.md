**What it shows.** Coded-factor coefficients of the full quadratic models, divided by the standard deviation of each response, with 95% confidence intervals; orange points have p below 0.05.

**How to read it.** A bar that crosses zero is not distinguishable from no effect. Longer bars are larger effects once all factors are scaled to the same coded range (-1 to +1).

**Key takeaway.** The blend term (A) is significant for all five responses; for tenacity the other significant terms are B2, C2. For tenacity the blend effect moving from the 30 to the 70 level is 1.98 cN/tex (95% CI 1.57 to 2.38), while process speeds matter far less.
